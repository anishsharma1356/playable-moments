Independent reconstruction of Paul Morphy against the Duke of Brunswick and Count Isouard, Paris 1858. Morphy moves the white queen from b3 to b8 with check. The black knight moves from d7 to b8 and captures it. The white rook moves from d1 to d8: checkmate. The vacated d7 square clears the rook's file. The bishop on g5 protects d8 along f6 and e7, and covers the king's e7 escape. The narration explains piece coordination rather than only material value.
